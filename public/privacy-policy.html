Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

Memory Data: We store your memory weaves, training sessions, and progress data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to sync or backup.

Usage Analytics: We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app, including feature usage, session duration, and performance metrics. This data cannot be linked to your identity.

Device Information: We collect basic device information (browser type, screen size, operating system) to optimize the app experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Memory Training: Your memory data is used solely to provide personalized training experiences
  • Progress Tracking: We analyze your training patterns to optimize spaced retrieval schedules
  • App Improvement: Anonymous usage data helps us enhance features and fix bugs
  • Research (Optional): With explicit consent, anonymized data may contribute to memory research

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: All personal memory data is stored locally on your device using encrypted browser storage.

No Cloud Storage: We do not store your memory content on our servers unless you explicitly enable cloud backup.

Encryption: All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal memory data with third parties. Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics may be shared with:

  • Academic researchers studying memory and cognition (with your consent)
  • Healthcare partners for clinical validation studies (with your consent)
  • Service providers who help us operate the app (under strict confidentiality agreements)

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can export all your data at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete all your data permanently
  • Portability: You can transfer your data to other apps
  • Opt-out: You can disable analytics and research participation

6. Healthcare Compliance

For users in healthcare settings, we maintain additional protections:

  • HIPAA-compliant data handling procedures
  • Enhanced encryption for clinical environments
  • Audit logs for healthcare provider access
  • Business Associate Agreements available for healthcare organizations

7. International Users

GDPR Compliance: EU users have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to be forgotten and data portability.

Data Residency: Data processing occurs in your region when possible to comply with local data protection laws.

8. Children's Privacy

Eidolon is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of any material changes to this privacy policy through the app and via email if you've provided one. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

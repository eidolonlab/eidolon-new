## Design Guidelines
- **Brand colors:** Indigo/purple gradient (#4f46e5 to #6366f1)
- **Typography:** Clean, readable fonts (SF Pro for iOS, Roboto for Android)
- **Layout:** Consistent spacing and professional appearance
- **Quality:** High-resolution, crisp screenshots
- **Content:** Realistic but anonymized data

## Required Sizes
### iOS
- **iPhone 6.7":** 1290 x 2796 pixels (iPhone 14 Pro Max)
- **iPhone 6.5":** 1242 x 2688 pixels (iPhone 14 Plus)
- **iPad Pro 12.9":** 2048 x 2732 pixels

### Android
- **Phone:** 1080 x 1920 pixels (minimum)
- **Tablet:** 1920 x 1080 pixels (optional)

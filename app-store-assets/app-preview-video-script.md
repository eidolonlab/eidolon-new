# 🎬 Eidolon App Preview Video Script

## Video Specifications

### iOS App Store
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds
- **Resolution:** 1080p or higher
- **Format:** MOV or MP4
- **File size:** Maximum 500MB
- **Orientation:** Portrait (recommended for mobile apps)

### Google Play Store
- **Duration:** 30 seconds maximum
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum
- **Format:** MP4, MOV, or AVI
- **File size:** Maximum 100MB
- **Orientation:** Portrait or landscape

## Production Guidelines

### Visual Style
- **Clean, modern interface** showcasing app design
- **Smooth animations** and transitions
- **High-quality screen recordings** from actual app
- **Consistent branding** with Eidolon colors

### Audio
- **Professional voiceover** (clear, engaging tone)
- **Background music** (subtle, uplifting)
- **Sound effects** (minimal, app-appropriate)
- **Audio levels** properly balanced

### Text Overlays
- **Large, readable fonts** (minimum 24pt)
- **High contrast** against background
- **Consistent typography** with app design
- **Animated appearance** for engagement

## Video Testing

### Before Submission
- [ ] **Test on mobile devices** - how does it look on small screens?
- [ ] **Test without sound** - is the message clear visually?
- [ ] **Test loading speed** - does it load quickly on slow connections?
- [ ] **Test accessibility** - are captions needed?

### A/B Testing Ideas
- **Different hooks** - various opening messages
- **Feature emphasis** - highlight different core features
- **User types** - focus on students vs. professionals vs. seniors
- **Benefit focus** - memory improvement vs. goal achievement vs. confidence

## Localization Considerations

### Text Overlays
- **Keep text minimal** for easier localization
- **Use universal symbols** where possible
- **Plan for text expansion** in other languages
- **Consider cultural differences** in messaging

### Voiceover
- **Script translation** for key markets
- **Native speaker recording** for authenticity
- **Cultural adaptation** of examples and scenarios

## Performance Optimization

### File Size Optimization
- **Compress video** without quality loss
- **Optimize for mobile** viewing
- **Test download speeds** on various connections
- **Consider multiple quality versions**

### Platform-Specific Optimization
- **iOS:** Optimize for Retina displays
- **Android:** Test on various screen densities
- **Both:** Ensure smooth playback on older devices
